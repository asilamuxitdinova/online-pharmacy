Prescriptions

Some medicines (antibiotics, blood pressure and diabetes medicine, strong painkillers) need a prescription. In the catalog they have "requires_prescription": true.

  1. Upload the prescription details with POST /prescriptions: doctor, clinic, date issued and diagnosis.
  2. A pharmacist reviews it (pending_review → approved or rejected), usually within 2 working hours.
  3. Give the approved prescription's id as prescription_id when you order.

A prescription is valid for one year from the day it was issued and cannot be dated in the future. A rejection always comes with a note explaining why.

Diagnoses are medical information. Staff see them; nobody else does.

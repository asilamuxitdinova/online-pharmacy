Error codes

Every error has the same shape:

{"error": {"status": 422, "code": "validation_failed", "message": "…",
           "fields": [{"field": "items[0].quantity", "code": "out_of_range", "message": "must be between 1 and 10"}],
           "required_role": "staff", "retry_after": 30, "details": {…}, "request_id": "req_…"}}

code is stable; message is for people and follows the request's locale where a translation exists.

StatusCodeWhat to do
400invalid_jsonThe body is not valid JSON. Fix the syntax.
400unknown_fieldA field the API does not know (named in details.field). Remove it.
401token_missingSend a credential.
401token_invalidThe credential is malformed or signed with the wrong key. Sign in again.
401token_expiredRenew with the refresh token.
401session_endedThe sign-in was ended (sign-out, reuse detection, account deleted). Sign in again. details.reason says why.
401refresh_reusedA spent refresh token was replayed; the whole sign-in ended. Sign in again.
401invalid_credentialsWrong email or password. details.attempts_left counts down.
401code_invalid, code_expiredWrong or old one-time code. Ask for a new one.
401signature_invalid, signature_expiredRecompute the partner signature; check the clock.
402card_declined, insufficient_funds, card_expiredPayment refused; nothing was charged. Try another card.
403role_requiredThe caller's role is too low; required_role names the one needed. Do not retry.
403account_disabledAn administrator disabled the account. Do not retry.
403act_as_requiredStaff must send X-Act-As to change a customer's order.
404*_not_foundNo such record, or it belongs to someone else.
409order_already_paid, order_paid, order_not_editable, order_cancelledThe order's state does not allow this action. details.status gives the state.
409insufficient_stockLower the quantity to details.available or pick another product.
409refund_already_decided, prescription_already_reviewedSomeone already decided.
422validation_failedFix every field in fields at once, then retry.
422idempotency_key_reusedUse a new Idempotency-Key for a different request.
429too_many_attemptsWait Retry-After seconds before trying again.
500internal_errorA bug on our side. Report the request_id.
503simulated_failureOnly from the diagnostics endpoint, on request. Retry after Retry-After.

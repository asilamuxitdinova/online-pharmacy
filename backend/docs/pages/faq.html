Frequently asked questions

Is delivery free?

Courier delivery is free when the products in your order cost 300 000 UZS or more. Below that it costs 25 000 UZS. Pickup from a branch is always free.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes, until it is paid. After payment, ask for a refund instead.

How fast will I get my money back?

Refunds up to 200 000 UZS are approved at once; larger ones are checked by a pharmacist first. After approval the money reaches Uzcard and Humo cards within 1 working day and Visa and Mastercard within 3 to 5 working days.

Why can't I order this medicine?

It needs a prescription. Upload one, wait for a pharmacist to approve it, then add its id to your order.

I typed the wrong password too many times.

Wait 5 minutes and try again, or sign in with a one-time code.

Do you store my card number?

No. Only the card brand and its last four digits are kept.

Which languages do you speak?

English, Russian and Uzbek.

Where is my order?

GET /orders/{id} shows its status. Paid orders in Tashkent arrive the same day if paid before 16:00, elsewhere the next working day.

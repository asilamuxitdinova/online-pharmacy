Payments

Pay an order with POST /orders/{id}/pay, using either a saved card ({"card_id"}) or a card used once ({"card": {"number", "exp_month", "exp_year", "cvc"}}).

Paying moves money. An assistant must get a clear yes from the customer, naming the order and the total, before calling it.

  • An order is charged at most once. Paying a paid order answers 409 order_already_paid.
  • A refused card answers 402 with card_declined or insufficient_funds. No money is taken and the order stays pending_payment.
  • Card numbers are never stored or returned. Only the brand and the last four digits are kept.

Accepted cards

Visa, Mastercard, Uzcard and Humo, 16 digits.

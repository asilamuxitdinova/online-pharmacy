Dorixona

Dorixona is an online pharmacy delivering medicines, vitamins and everyday health products across Uzbekistan. These pages explain how ordering, delivery, prescriptions, payments and refunds work, and document the Dorixona API for developers and partners.

Customers browse medicines, upload prescriptions, place orders, pay by card and ask for refunds. Our pharmacists review prescriptions and larger refunds. Administrators manage accounts.

  • Prescriptions: what needs one and how review works.
  • Payments and the refund policy, with amounts.

  • Error codes: every error the API returns and what to do about it.

Basics

All amounts are whole Uzbek soʻm (UZS), for example "price": 4000. Times are UTC in RFC 3339 format. Product names and many messages are available in English, Russian and Uzbek: send Locale: ru or Locale: uz (or the standard Accept-Language header).

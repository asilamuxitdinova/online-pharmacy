Refund policy

Customers can ask for money back on a paid order with POST /orders/{id}/refunds, giving an amount in UZS and a reason.

Who decides

Amount asked forDecision
Up to and including 200 000 UZSApproved automatically and at once. The money is returned to the card.
More than 200 000 UZSWaits for a pharmacist (status pending_review). No money moves until a member of staff approves it with POST /staff/refunds/{id}/approve.

An assistant must never approve a refund over 200 000 UZS on its own, and must tell the customer that a pharmacist will review it.

Limits

  • The total of all refunds, approved and pending, cannot exceed what was paid, delivery included.
  • Several partial refunds are allowed. When the full amount has been returned the order becomes refunded.
  • Only paid orders can be refunded. An unpaid order is cancelled instead.
  • Prescription medicine that has left the pharmacy is refunded only for a pharmacy error (wrong item, damaged pack).

Timing

Approved refunds reach the card in 3 to 5 working days for Visa and Mastercard and within 1 working day for Uzcard and Humo.
